EVALUATION DEADLINE POLICY - CONFIRMED BY MARWA, 25 NOVEMBER 2026

Set this in each developer's local .env (never commit .env):
EVALUATION_SLA_DAYS=7
Restart the application after changing the setting.

The agreed project/company policy is seven calendar days (168 hours), including weekends and holidays, from the first successful release of actual offers to the Evaluation Committee through ComplianceController.SendToCommittee. Publication/brief-only notifications do not start the period. UTC timestamps are stored.

EvaluationScheduleService already saves the start, SlaDays and due date once. Repeated release, rework and later setting changes retain the saved schedule. This policy does not require a new schema or migration. Do not backfill historical deadlines with guessed dates.

Completion is EvaluationReport.SubmittedAt, not an individual member's Ready or recommendation vote.
- No completion, now <= deadline: In progress.
- No completion, now > deadline: Overdue.
- Completion <= deadline: Completed on time.
- Completion > deadline: Completed late.
- Missing/invalid saved schedule/timing: Unknown.

Reports read the saved per-tender schedule, including its actual SlaDays; they do not impose today's setting retrospectively. The 7-day duration is the adopted project policy, not a duration explicitly specified by the amendment document (which requires evaluation-delay reporting).
